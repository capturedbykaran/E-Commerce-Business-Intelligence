# 🛒 E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics Platform

A complete end-to-end Business Intelligence project that transforms raw e-commerce transaction data into actionable business insights using **Python, MySQL, and Power BI**.
<sub>

The project covers the entire analytics pipeline—from data cleaning and feature engineering to SQL-based business analysis and interactive Power BI dashboards—helping businesses improve sales performance, customer retention, logistics efficiency, and strategic decision-making. The project is based on a real-world e-commerce dataset and includes comprehensive analysis, dashboards, and business recommendations.

</sub>


## 📌 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to build a complete Business Intelligence solution by:

- Cleaning and transforming raw e-commerce data
- Performing feature engineering
- Designing a relational database
- Conducting SQL-based business analysis
- Creating interactive Power BI dashboards
- Providing business recommendations based on analytical findings

# 📊 Dashboards

The project contains **5 interactive Power BI dashboards**:

## 1. Overview Dashboard

Provides an executive summary of the business with KPIs including revenue, orders, customers, AOV, payment distribution, regional performance, and sales trends.

---

## 2. Customer Analytics Dashboard

Analyzes customer acquisition, retention, spending behaviour, satisfaction, and regional customer distribution to support customer-focused decision-making.

---

## 3. Product & Sales Dashboard

Shows product performance, revenue trends, category analysis, pricing insights, and sales patterns to support inventory and pricing strategies.

---

## 4. Seller Analytics Dashboard

Evaluates seller revenue, order volume, ratings, delivery performance, and geographical distribution to improve marketplace efficiency.

---

## 5. Logistics Dashboard

Monitors shipping performance, delivery delays, average delivery time, and logistics bottlenecks to improve operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

Some major insights identified during the analysis include:

- Most customers are one-time buyers, indicating opportunities to improve customer retention.
- Credit cards are the most preferred payment method.
- Revenue is concentrated in a few product categories and regions.
- Delivery delays significantly reduce customer satisfaction.
- A small group of sellers contributes a large share of total revenue.
- Sales exhibit seasonal demand patterns across months and weekdays.
